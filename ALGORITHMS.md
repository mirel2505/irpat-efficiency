# Algorithms and validation contract
All formulas are engineering estimates unless their inputs and assumptions support a measurement. Missing data -> unavailable; do not clamp errors to plausible values.

## Thermal
For room i, e_i = time-weighted measured temperature - target. Common error = mean of e_i (area weighted only with complete suitable area data); individual residual = e_i - common error. Preserve individual targets; report coverage and persistent deviations, not single sample. Positive common error (rooms warmer than target) suggests reviewing a lower supply temperature; negative suggests reviewing a higher supply temperature, both in heating and cooling. For cooling, any lower supply temperature requires independent condensation and equipment-limit verification. These directions are hypotheses, not automatic commands. Local warm room: reduce heating or increase cooling only after validation; cold room opposite. No exact flow increment from ambient temperature alone.
Confirmation: configurable consecutive full days (default proposed 5, range 3–7), coverage, time in tolerance, drift and no destabilizing interventions. Thresholds for coverage and time in tolerance require validation; do not claim whole-season balance from one weather interval. Evaluate 24/48/72 h after intervention if comparable. Do not recommend when ON/OFF or active zone cycling invalidates assumptions. 
## Hydraulic
Convert to consistent units: 1 m³/h = 1000/60 L/min. Relative share = circuit flow / relevant manifold total; normalization to reference total: Dn = Dc * Dref / Dtotal. Example: 0.85 L/min at 1.10 m³/h corresponds to 1.20545 L/min at 1.56 m³/h; relative shares 4.64% and 4.62% for prior 1.20 L/min. Only comparable topology, fluid, valve states, stable measurement conditions and denominators; source flow not necessarily manifold flow. Sum all simultaneous loop readings only if complete. Zero total means unavailable. Normalization is not a hydraulic law. No assumption equal flow is optimal. Theoretical water flow L/min ~ P(W)/(69.8*deltaT(K)) only for appropriate water properties and known design load.
## Energy
P(W) = density(kg/m³)*cp(J/kg/K)*flow(m³/s)*(supply-return)(K). Signed heat to circuit; negative for cooling/defrost depending on boundaries, use mode-specific energy conventions. Use fluid-specific properties, validate units, time alignment and meter resets. Do not substitute water coefficients for unknown glycol. Integrate power over valid intervals only; distinguish incomplete data. COP = delivered net thermal kWh / electrical kWh for same period/system boundary; denominator > 0. Fuel efficiency needs measured useful heat and fuel energy on stated LHV/HHV basis. Never infer thermal power from pressure alone.
H_apparent(W/K) = 1000*Qnet(kWh)/integral(Tinside-Toutside)dt(h) for comparable heating periods, positive degree-hours; not a certified loss coefficient, affected by storage and gains. Natural cooling slope = temperature drop / elapsed hours; residual floor heat and gains limit interpretation.
## Condensation
Magnus approximation dewpoint: alpha = ln(RH/100)+17.62*T/(243.12+T); Td=243.12*alpha/(17.62-alpha). Validate domain and humidity; compare with measured relevant surface, or separately label water-temperature proxy. Unavailable humidity/temperature => unknown, not safe. Independent hardware protections required. Provisional review margin 2°C, maximum report age 900 seconds and timestamp skew 300 seconds. Treat missing measurements as unknown; never certify a zone as safe based on a supply-water proxy or a few surface readings.
## Validation
Unit tests: missing/invalid/NaN, zero denominator, unit conversion, signed heat, data gaps, duplicate circuit association, counter resets, modes, multiple manifolds, varying flow, mixed sensors, daylight saving, persistence/migration, heating/cooling, no hardware service calls. Golden examples are synthetic only. Algorithms require real-world comparison before stable release.
